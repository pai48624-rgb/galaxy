Seoraksan National Park spans Sokcho, Yangyang and Inje in Gangwon Province. Its highest peak, Daecheongbong, rises to 1,708 m, the third highest in South Korea after Hallasan and Jirisan. The Korea National Park Service lists 15 official trails, which it rates as 8 easy, 2 moderate and 5 hard. They range from a 30-minute walk to a full day on the ridges, so it pays to choose your entrance and your trail before you set off.

The shortest way to the summit is the Osaek trail (4 hours), but it is steep. For a first visit, a walk of two hours or less is a safer choice: Biryong Falls or Ulsanbawi Rock from Seorak-dong, or Yongso Falls from Osaek. Park data lists five mountain shelters. Whether they are open, and whether you need to book, changes from season to season, so check the Korea National Park Service reservation site before you go.

If you need a doctor

The nearest emergency departments to the park entrances are Onjae Hospital (regional emergency institution, 6.3 km) and Sokcho Medical Center (regional emergency centre, 8.3 km). Pharmacies are 5.8 to 6.0 km away. If you are injured on the mountain, call 119 and read out the number on the nearest national location marker on the trail. It helps rescuers find you quickly.

Where to stay

There are 53 licensed places to stay within 3 km of the park's visitor centres and ranger stations: 27 motels, 18 serviced residences, 5 hotels, 1 condo resort and 2 others. Guesthouses and pensions are licensed separately and are not counted here. The nearest campsite is Yangyang Osaek Hueland, 5.5 km from the Osaek side.
