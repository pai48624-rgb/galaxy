 Your team doesn’t need another status meeting. It needs to know what changed, what’s stuck, and who can help.
 0 1 Give progress a place to live When updates are scattered across chats and calls, someone has to piece the story together. One short, shared note gives everyone the same starting point.
 0 2 Start with three questions What moved forward? What needs a decision? What happens next? Add an owner and a date to anything that needs action.
 0 3 Try it for one week Share your update before Monday’s check-in. Use the meeting for decisions that need a conversation. If there aren’t any, give the time back.

## The open-source platform for AI agents

AutoGPT lets you build, deploy, and run AI agents that carry out complete workflows. Describe an outcome in plain English or shape every step in the visual builder, then run the agent on demand, on a schedule, or from a trigger.
