 Enterprise AI transformation is a CEO top-down mandate 
 AI transformation is a top-down executive mandate, not a series of isolated pilots. Its value comes from integrating strategy, scenarios, data, models, and organization so AI becomes the enterprise's decision-making core.
 Advisory-led transformation, fewer detours
 Co-create the AI transformation blueprint with the CEO and leadership team, aligning the roadmap, technology stack, product portfolio, and delivery milestones with ROI from day one.
 Ontology modeling for business understanding
 Model people, assets, processes, events, and decision logic as computable business objects, so AI understands context instead of merely reading data.
 FDE co-creation to accelerate delivery
 Forward Deployed Engineers (FDE) go deep into frontline operations, working with customer teams to break down requirements, connect data, build prototypes, and validate deployment.
 Closed-loop operations for continuous improvement
 Turn real-world usage into data, models, and business knowledge, so AI capabilities evolve with the business and inform strategic iteration.

 About Us
 01.AI was founded in Beijing in May 2023 under the leadership of Dr. Kai-Fu Lee and became a unicorn within six months. Driving the frontier of generative and agentic AI technology, 01.AI focuses on the booming opportunity of global industry AI transformation. The company builds full-stack enterprise solutions, industry agent deployments and applications, as well as industry and sovereign model training. With strong executive and leadership partnerships driving AI transformation through a top-down mandate, 01.AI is scaling enterprise and sovereign AI deployments globally.

 一号位工程，驱动企业 AI 数智化转型 
 AI 转型的价值不在于多做几个试点，而在于由一号位牵引，把战略、组织、场景、数据与模型打通，让 AI 真正进入企业决策中枢。
 咨询牵引，少走弯路
 与一号位共绘 AI 顶层蓝图，明确路线图、技术栈、产品矩阵与交付里程碑，让 AI 从一开始对准经营目标和 ROI。

 把企业中的人、物、流程、事件和决策逻辑抽象为可计算对象，让模型理解业务地图，不只是读取数据。

 前沿部署工程师（FDE）深入业务一线，与客户团队共同拆解需求、接入数据、搭建原型、部署验证。
 闭环运营，持续增效
 把场景实践沉淀为数据、模型和业务经验，让 AI 能力随业务演进，并持续反哺企业战略迭代。

 关于我们
 零一万物成立于2023年5月，总部位于北京，由李开复博士领衔创立，成立六个月即晋级独角兽公司。作为 AI 2.0 与智能体变革的最前沿推动者，零一万物着眼 AI 时代全球的产业智能化转型契机，深耕全栈企业级解决方案、行业智能体落地应用及轻量化产业大模型训练。公司以“一号位工程”为核心纽带，全面发力全球企业 AI 与主权 AI 规模化落地，致力于将前沿的大模型技术转化为新质生产力，深度赋能实体经济高质量发展。
