 FAQ
 Common questions.
 View all FAQs → What if my AI hallucinates? Nectry blocks operations that fall outside your rules before they execute. The model can be wrong without gaining permission to take an unauthorized action.
 Does Nectry integrate with tools our team already uses? Yes. Nectry supports popular services including Google Workspace, GitHub, Slack, and HubSpot, along with tools that provide a REST or MCP API.
 Can a non-technical person use Nectry? Yes. Teams can configure Nectry by describing what they want, then review the exact configuration visually and in plain language.
