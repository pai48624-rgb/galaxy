 Making real-time data accessible for AI.
 We’re on a mission to help people move faster, stay grounded, and do more with AI via Web Search APIs.
 THE PROBLEM
 LLMs are only as good as the data they are trained on.
 Real-time data isn't always factored in.
 Foundational models still fall short when it comes to their training cutoffs—limiting the full benefits of agents built on top of them.
 Disconnected data.
 There’s no easy way to combine internal knowledge with real-time external sources, like the web or live news.
 Untrustworthy outputs.
 Hallucinations, un-cited claims, and off-base responses make results unreliable — and impossible to validate.
 Rigid tooling.
 No single foundational model delivers all the reasoning, retrieval, or domain coverage enterprises need.
 Slow adoption.
 Many teams lack the expertise needed to deploy, manage, and scale AI systems quickly and with confidence.
 Security gaps.
 Most AI tools don’t offer the security enterprises need to protect sensitive data, limiting their ability to adopt AI with confidence.
