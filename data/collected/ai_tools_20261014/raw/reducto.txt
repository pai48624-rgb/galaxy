 Introducing r-1: Reducto’s new SOTA document parsing model About Reducto
 Reducto is the agentic document platform for leading AI teams who demand enterprise performance at scale. The platform brings together parsing, extraction, splitting, classification, and editing so teams can turn documents into structured, citation-ready data and complete document workflows.
 Reducto is built for AI-native companies and document-heavy enterprises. Teams at Harvey, Scale AI, and Vanta use Reducto, and the platform has processed more than 5 billion pages. Its products handle PDFs, scans, spreadsheets, slides, handwriting, tables, charts, and other file types without requiring templates or retraining.
 The platform includes the Reducto API and SDKs for developers, Reducto Studio for building and deploying document pipelines without code, a CLI for terminal workflows, and an MCP server for connecting document tools to AI agents. Start at Studio , read the developer documentation at docs.reducto.ai , or point your AI agent to the Agent skill .
 Reducto supports production requirements with SOC 2 and HIPAA compliance, zero data retention agreements on the Growth tier and above, enterprise support, custom SLAs, and hosted, VPC, on-premises, and air-gapped deployment options. Teams can get started right away with 15,000 free credits, and volume discounts and custom pricing are available on the Growth tier and above as you scale. For questions, contact support@reducto.ai or use the contact page . Explore the full platform at reducto.ai .
