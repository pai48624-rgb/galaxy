 Salesforce AI Research is the engine behind AI innovation at Salesforce. We advance novel capabilities, trust frameworks, and agentic systems that shape how enterprises work.
 We work around five macro trends that are shaping the future of work
 Self-Evolving Models & Agents
 From static deployment to continuous self-improvement. Agents iteratively refine their own objectives, strategies, and tool use through experience.
 Operational Intelligence
 Optimizing analytical insight and autonomous action; we’re embedding AI-driven decision-making directly into real-world workflows and infrastructure.
 Ambient Intelligence
 Context-aware systems that perceive, reason, and act across physical and digital environments — shifting AI from a reactive tool to a persistent, anticipatory presence.
 Multi-Agent Ecosystems
 Agents that coordinate, negotiate, and collaborate — exploring how agent interoperability and orchestration drives business impact.
 Simulation Environments
 Realistic, simulated worlds for measuring, evaluating, and training autonomous agents across thousands of scenarios before real-world deployment.

 광고 쿠키 
 이 쿠키는 회사의 광고 협력사가 회사 사이트에 설정한 것입니다. 해당 협력사는 귀하의 관심사에 대한 프로파일을 만들고 다른 사이트에서도 관련 광고를 표시하기 위해 이 쿠키를 사용합니다. 이 쿠키는 귀하의 브라우저와 기기를 식별함으로써 동작합니다. 이 쿠키를 허용하지 않으면 다른 웹사이트에서 회사가 제공하는 맞춤형 광고를 경험할 수 없습니다.
