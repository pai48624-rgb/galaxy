 Our Story
 In finance, rigor is everything. A single position can touch thousands of documents, dozens of data sources, and a research process that runs through models, memos, and presentations. Being slightly wrong costs the same as being completely wrong, so a system that's right most of the time isn't one institutional finance can use.
 Firms must encode their own processes and judgment into every system they build. We've spent more than six years building for that, alongside people who spent their careers as bankers, investors, lawyers, consultants, and operators at the world's leading institutions.
 The result is an intelligence platform built for how finance operates.
