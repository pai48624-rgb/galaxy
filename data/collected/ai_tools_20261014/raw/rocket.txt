 QUESTIONS WORTH ANSWERING
 How do credits work?
 Credits are the currency for everything you do on Rocket — every Solve, Build task and every competitor tracked draws from your credit balance. The Free plan includes 20 one-time credits. Paid plans include a set amount of recurring monthly credits. When you need more, you can buy additional credits at any time without upgrading your plan.
 Can I try Rocket for free?
 Yes. The Free plan gives you one-time 20 credits — no credit card required. You get access to all Build capabilities, public tasks and personalized business intelligence. When you're ready for more, paid plans start at $25 per month.
 Why no per-seat pricing?
 Because teams shouldn't have to choose between collaboration and cost. Every Rocket plan includes unlimited team members. You pay for what you use — not for how many people are using it.
 Can I buy more credits if I run out?
 Yes. Additional credits are available on every plan at any time. You're never blocked mid-project because of a monthly limit.
 Do I have to commit to a minimum credit purchase every month?
 No. The Free plan includes 20 one-time credits. Paid plans include a set amount of credits each month covered by your platform fee. Buying additional credits is entirely optional. If you use what's included, you pay nothing extra. If you need more, you buy only what you need.
 If I don't use all my credits in a month, do they roll over?
 Yes. Unused credits carry forward as long as your subscription is active. Nothing is wasted.
 Which plan is best for me?
 Start with what you need to accomplish. Free plan gives you 20 one-time credits at no cost. If you're building production-ready websites, landing pages, web apps, or mobile apps regularly, the Pro plan at $25/month is the best starting point with 100 monthly credits. For individuals and teams that need higher usage and consultant-grade solutions on demand, Rocket at $50/month includes 250 monthly credits. If you need the full suite for fast-moving teams — including competitive intelligence and maximum usage capacity — Booster at $250/month delivers the most comprehensive setup with 1500 monthly credits.
 What's the difference between Solve, Build, and Intelligence?
 Build generates production-grade websites, landing pages, web apps, and mobile apps. Solve takes any business question or situation and returns a complete structured solution — findings, evidence, and a clear recommendation, ready to act on. Intelligence monitors every public platform your competitors operate on, continuously, and tells you what each signal means for your business.
 Do I need to commit long-term?
 No. Paid plans are available with monthly or annual billing, and you can cancel anytime. Annual billing saves 20%.
 Do I need a credit card to get started?
 No. The Free plan requires no credit card. You only need payment details when upgrading to a paid plan.
 Can I switch plans or cancel anytime?
 Yes. Change plans or cancel from your account settings whenever you need to.
 How do refunds work?
 If something isn't right, contact our support team and we'll sort it out.

 GETTING STARTED
 Introduction
 Introduction to Rocket.new, the vibe solutioning platform that combines Solve research, Build for web and mobile apps, and Intelligence monitoring.
 Rocket.new is a vibe solutioning platform that combines strategic research, AI app building, and competitive intelligence into a single product. Validate your idea, build the product, and track your competitors without switching tools. 
 ​ 
 Three pillars, one platform 
 Solve
 Validate ideas, run market research, create PRDs, and get structured reports with data, insights, and recommendations. 
 Build
 Build production-ready web apps, mobile apps, SaaS products, e-commerce stores, and landing pages from natural language. 
 Intelligence
 Monitor competitors continuously. Get automated daily briefs, pricing change alerts, and trend signals. 
 Pillar What it produces 
 Solve Structured reports with data, insights, and actionable recommendations 
 Build Production-ready Next.js web apps and Flutter mobile apps 
 Intelligence Continuous signals delivered to a live dashboard in your sidebar 
 Each pillar works independently. Use one, two, or all three depending on what you need. 
 ​ 
 What you can build 
 Web apps
 SaaS dashboards, internal tools, marketplaces, AI-powered tools, customer portals. 
 Mobile apps
 iOS and Android apps built with Flutter, ready for App Store and Google Play submission. 
 Landing pages and sites
 Marketing sites, portfolio pages, and product launch pages with custom domains. 
 E-commerce stores
 Full online stores with product listings, cart, checkout, and payment integrations. 
 ​ 
 How capabilities connect 
 The three pillars share context and feed into each other. 
 1
 Research informs building
 Run a Solve task to validate your idea and understand the market. Use those insights to scope your Build task with full context already in place. 
 2
 Monitoring triggers action
 Intelligence surfaces a competitor change: a pricing shift, a new feature, a hiring spike. Use that signal to start a Solve analysis or update your product in Build. 
 3
 Building reveals research needs
 While building, you realize you need data on user preferences or market sizing. Create a Solve task, get structured findings, and apply them directly.
