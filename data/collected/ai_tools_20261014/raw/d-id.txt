 ​​ Humanizing digital communication  
 Shaping the age of digital humans
 At D-ID, we believe communication is most effective when it feels human. Our platform helps organizations deliver clear, consistent, and high-impact communication through professional, multilingual avatar videos and interactive experiences with visual agents. 
 Organizations worldwide use D-ID to elevate customer experience, streamline support, accelerate training and onboarding, enhance learning, and strengthen brand messaging across channels, ensuring communication that remains consistent, effective, and easy to scale. 
 About Us
 Established in 2017 and backed by tier-1 venture investors, D-ID has been at the forefront of generative AI for nearly a decade—pioneering the technologies that now define the emerging era of Digital Humans. Our self-service studio and API have powered more than 200 million avatar videos and attracted a global community of over 280,000 developers building AI-driven solutions for customer experience, training, marketing, and communication. 
 In 2025, D-ID expanded its leadership through the acquisition of simpleshow, uniting two innovators in AI video creation and interactive communication. This milestone accelerated our vision of delivering a unified Digital Human platform, combining enterprise-grade video creation with real-time, humanlike interaction. Today, leading organizations including PepsiCo, Fidelity, J.P. Morgan, SoftBank, NTT, Deutsche Telekom, PwC, Deloitte, Burda Media, AXA Insurance, and Gameloft rely on D-ID to create impactful, scalable digital experiences powered by expressive, intelligent Digital Humans. 
 Pioneers in interactive avatars and AI video creation 
 Our technology, available via a self-service studio, API, or integrations blends advanced generative models with expressive avatar animation, automated narration, and multilingual capabilities to produce polished, on-brand video content at scale. Building on this foundation, our real-time visual agents add natural speech, conversational intelligence, and task execution, creating interactive, humanlike experiences that adapt instantly to each user.

 Gil Perry

 Gil is the Chief Executive Officer of D-ID. He was among the first developers in Generative AI, working in the space since it emerged in 2014, developing the first algorithm in the world to protect against face recognition technology. Prior to founding D-ID, he served in various technical and business roles in leading global tech companies, including Meerkat / Houseparty, where he was a senior software engineer.  
 Gil has served in an elite intelligence unit of the IDF. He holds a B.Sc. degree in Computer Science from Tel Aviv University, with expertise in computer vision, and Deep Learning.
