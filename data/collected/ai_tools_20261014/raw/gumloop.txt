Let anyone at your company build agents with any AI model and any integration, while IT controls access and spend.
 I'm aware this sounds very hand-wavy as a description, so let me go line by line to explain what I mean.
 “Multiplayer AI agent builder”
 You can build agents collaboratively with your coworkers. Everyone can contribute to improvements, new skills and can add new integrations.
 One agent can be used by any number of people in Slack, Teams, email or in the Gumloop interface. Several people can also interact in a single agent thread.
 “Build agents with any AI model and any integration”
 You can choose what model powers your agents. Anything from the frontier models to the open weight options that are as much as 99% cheaper.
 We have over 300 integrations natively supported on Gumloop and give you the ability to connect any external MCP or deploy your own custom MCPs on Gumloop.
 “While IT controls access and spend”
 Every observability or access control feature an IT leader could ask for is available on our enterprise tier. Role based access control, audit logging, observability via MCP gateway, MCP policy enforcement, analytics across activity and spend, model restriction, VPC on any provider.
 These are the six beliefs we're building the product around.
 I. You should be in control of your AI spend.
 Becoming AI-native doesn't mean spend has to go up and to the right forever. Admins deserve a rollout they can forecast, cap, and justify.
 How we make it real:
 Any model Model routing across frontier, open-source, and proprietary models means no single vendor's pricing ever holds you hostage. 
 Agents that get cheaper over time Gumloop agents reflect on and review their own work regularly, hunting for ways to run more efficiently. 
 Spend you can see Detailed dashboards show exactly where every dollar goes across agents, users, and the organization as a whole. 
 Limits Per-user and per-agent spend caps mean there is never an outlier spender. Ever. 
 Spend projections We analyze your usage patterns and show you exactly how your spend will scale over time. 
 II. You should own your agent's data.
 Your agents run on your infrastructure. Their traces are your record. You hold the lock and the key, not your vendor.
 How we make it real:
 Your cloud Deploy Gumloop in your own VPC. 
 Your database Through VPC, you own storage outright. That means owning every agent trace, forever. 
 Your choice of intelligence Frontier, open-source, or your own proprietary models. You decide what powers your agents. 
 Your keys, your proxies Route your agents' intelligence through your own API keys and your own LLM proxies, and control every dollar behind them. 
 III. You should see exactly how agents use your company's data.
 Every agent runs through a managed authentication layer your security team controls. Every action is logged and accounted for, or blocked if it crosses a line your admins drew. No black boxes.
