 Frequently asked questions
 What is LM Studio Bionic? LM Studio Bionic combines local AI features with optional cloud inference.
 Can I use Bionic for free? Yes. The Free plan includes Bionic Agent and local model support.
 Can I continue running models locally? Yes. Bionic supports local models alongside optional cloud models.
 What does zero data retention mean? Zero data retention means your prompts and responses are not stored by the provider.
 Does web search use Zero Data Retention (ZDR)? Yes. Web search uses Zero Data Retention (ZDR). However, querying and processing data from the web always carries its own privacy and security risks. Be mindful of sensitive information in your queries and treat web content as untrusted.
 What are cloud credits for? Cloud credits cover usage beyond the allowance included with your plan.
 Do you offer plans for teams? We currently offer centralized billing for teams using inference credits. Create an organization and invite your team. Subscription plans for teams coming soon.
 Can I change my plan later? You can manage your plan from your dashboard.
