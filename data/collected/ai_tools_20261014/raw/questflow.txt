 Questions, answered
 What's the difference between Free, Pro, and Max? Free is for retail traders reading the market with AI. Pro is the AI Finance Agent: unlock all flagship models, get deep research and official signals, and upload your own custom skills. Max scales it with 5× usage, a persistent cloud computer, and your own fund. Each tier includes everything below it.
 Pro and Max share the same models. What's extra on Max? Max includes everything in Pro, and adds 5× the usage, a persistent cloud computer, and the ability to launch your own fund.
 What is the AI Finance Agent on Pro? On Pro you unlock all flagship models, get deep research and official signals with live push, and can upload your own custom skills and plugins, turning Questflow into a trading agent built around your own logic and data.
 What models do I get on each tier? Free runs on efficient open models like DeepSeek, MiniMax and Gemini. Pro and Max unlock all flagship models, including Claude, GPT and Kimi, switchable anytime.
 What is the persistent cloud computer on Max? A cloud runtime that keeps working even when your computer is off. You can customize signal monitoring and order execution on it, so nothing stops when you step away.
 How much do I save with annual billing? Annual billing saves 30% versus the standard monthly price. Pro is $13.99/month ($167.88/year), saving $72/year; Max is $69.99/month ($839.88/year), saving $360/year.
 How does the Pro first-month offer work? Eligible new Pro subscribers pay $4.99 for the first month with card or Apple Pay, then $19.99/month unless canceled. The offer does not apply to annual Pro, Max, or crypto payments.
 Can I upgrade or cancel anytime? Yes. Upgrade anytime and unused value credits toward the higher tier. Cancel whenever, and access lasts through your billing period.
 What payment methods do you accept? Card or Apple Pay via Stripe, or crypto (USDC / USDT). Cancel anytime.
