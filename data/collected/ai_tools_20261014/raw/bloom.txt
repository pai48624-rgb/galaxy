 Welcome to Bloom
 Copy page Copy page 
 Build and ship cross-platform apps with scalable database and auth built in, no coding required.
 Copy page Copy page 
 Describe what you want to build, and Bloom turns it into a real, cross-platform app complete with a native UI, scalable database, authentication, and external API support. No boilerplate, no backend setup, just a working product from the first prompt. 
 Every app you build in Bloom is yours. The underlying code is fully exportable and GitHub-syncable, so you’re never locked in. Whether you’re validating an idea solo or shipping something your whole team depends on, Bloom is built to grow with you. 
 Share your app the moment it’s ready with an instant link or QR code. No App Store review required. When you’re ready to go further, Bloom supports publishing to the App Store and Google Play. 
 Truly full-stack
 Build both frontend and backend logic with one unified workflow. 
 Instant sharing
 Launch your app and share instantly with a link or QR code. 
 Flexible from day one
 Start simple, customize as you grow, or export your code when ready. 
 ​ 
 Who Bloom is for 
 Bloom empowers founders, students, developers, creatives, teams, and makers who want to go from idea to app, fast. 
 Founders & Entrepreneurs
 Validate your MVP in days, not months. Test product market fit with real users before investing in a full development team. Build a waitlist app with a working backend, spin up a marketplace prototype, or launch a subscription tool to gauge demand. 
 Product Teams
 Ship internal tools, prototypes, and customer facing apps without backend bottlenecks. Build admin dashboards, feedback collectors, or customer portals and iterate on them in real time. Use Bloom for rapid iteration, then sync with GitHub to integrate into your existing engineering workflow. 
 Students
 Build real projects for class, hackathons, or your portfolio without needing a dev background. Create a campus events app, a study group coordination tool, or a polished capstone project that actually runs on your phone. Bloom handles the backend so you can focus on your idea and ship something you’re proud of. 
 Developers
 Prototype faster, scaffold backends, or hand off working apps to clients. Spin up a quick demo with auth and a database already wired, experiment with new product ideas without the setup overhead, or use Bloom as a starting point before moving to your own stack. Export to GitHub at any time and keep building in your preferred environment. 
 Creators & Designers
 Turn your app concepts into working prototypes without learning to code. Build a portfolio app, a fan community, or a content scheduling tool and bring your designs to life. Focus on user experience and design while Bloom handles the technical infrastructure.
