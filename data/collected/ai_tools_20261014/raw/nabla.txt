 Bringing clinicians back to patients
 Nabla builds the clinical intelligence layer that fits seamlessly into existing workflows, helping healthcare organizations improve clinician experience, deepen patient relationships, and deliver care more efficiently.
 Millions of clinical encounters supported
 Deployed across health systems and medical groups
 Built by AI pioneers and practicing clinicians
 What we build
 Nabla delivers a unified clinical AI platform used by healthcare organizations across specialties and care settings.
 Ambient AI documentation that captures clinical conversations and generates structured notes
 AI-powered dictation and clinical workflow tools embedded directly within EHR systems
 Agentic AI capabilities designed to support multi-step clinical and administrative tasks
 Enterprise-grade infrastructure built for secure deployment across health systems 
 Our vision
 The future of healthcare is human-centered and AI-enabled
 AI should strengthen—not replace—the human connection at the core of care. Clinical AI is evolving beyond documentation to support the full care journey, from preparation to coordination and follow-up.  
Nabla is building toward a future where AI works quietly in the background, improving outcomes while restoring time, focus, and satisfaction to the practice of medicine.
