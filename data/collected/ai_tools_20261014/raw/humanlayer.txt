HumanLayer - The multiplayer control plane for your software factory 
 The Multiplayer Coding Agent Workspace 
 HumanLayer is a multiplayer coding agent IDE and cloud that works seamlessly from your workstation to anywhere you want to run background agents.
 It brings agent sessions, plan artifacts, and code diffs together in one place for your team to collaborate and ship.
 HumanLayer ships composable pieces to meet software factory builders where they already are . Bring your own compute, development environment, and agent harness, or run the HumanLayer stack on your workstation or remote workers.

 Comment-Driven Design Reviews 
 Inline comments on design documents. Your whole team — humans and Agents — collaborating in real-time. Catch mistakes before they become 2,000 lines of code.
 Why not just use a Notion or Google Doc? Because there, your design doc is disconnected from the work. In HumanLayer, comments and decisions feed straight back to the agents — the doc is the interface to the code, not a separate artifact you hope someone reads before shipping.
