 You shouldn't pay more just because your data volume spikes
 For large organizations with higher data volumes, Airbyte provides enterprise plans that price on compute capacity. Scale your pipelines without scaling your bill.
 A better pricing model 
 The Pro and Enterprise Flex plans use capacity-based pricing built on Data Workers, the dedicated compute units that power your pipelines.
You scale by adding capacity, not by worrying about volume spikes. Costs stay predictable as your workloads grow.
 A note on volume-based pricing 
 For smaller teams with predictable data volumes, pay-per-use still makes sense. That's what Standard and Plus are for.
Pick the model that fits: fixed capacity on Pro and Enterprise Flex, or volume-based on Standard and Plus.

 FAQs
 Didn't find your answer? Please don't hesitate to reach out.
 What are credits?
 Airbyte credits are used to pay for Airbyte resources when you run a sync. Credits are used to unify pricing across multiple types of sources. Credit metering differs across source types, and for more details on this, please consult our documentation .
 When should I use Plus, and when should I use Standard?
 If you are consuming 40+ credits a month, Plus will typically be more affordable than Standard. It also comes with 15-min syncs, SSO, field renaming, and a 24 hr support SLA. If you value pay-as-you-go flexibility above all, Standard is the best play.
 Use our pricing calculator above to get a more detailed estimate and recommendation on the right plan.
 What plans does Airbyte offer, and how do they differ?
 Airbyte offers Standard, Plus, Pro, and Enterprise Flex. Standard is fully managed volume-based cloud. Plus includes everything in Standard, plus SSO, field renaming, 15-minute syncs, 2 workspaces, and a 24-hour support SLA, billed through credit packages. Pro features capacity based pricing and enterprise features such as RBAC and multiple workspaces. Enterprise Flex adds in-boundary and hybrid deployment. Click here to scroll up to the comparison matrix.
 What is capacity-based pricing (Data Workers)?
 Airbyte’s Pro and Enterprise Flex plans are priced by Data Workers, a unit of capacity that powers your pipelines. There are multiple variables that we use to determine the cost of a Data Worker.
 Each Data Worker can run multiple syncs concurrently; typically ~3 at once.
 This model gives you predictable spend, guaranteed performance, and no charges based on data volume.
 How is capacity-based pricing different from usage or volume-based? 
 Usage or volume-based pricing bills you by rows, GBs, or events. Costs grow as data grows and we know that not all data is equally valuable.
 Airbyte uses capacity-based pricing, so you can control spend up-front, avoid surprise bills and can scale only when needed, so your data can grow without inflating your bill.
 What is the Pro plan? 
 Pro includes everything in Plus, with capacity-based pricing, premium support, and enhanced platform features like RBAC, row filtering, hashing & encryption, and support for multiple data regions. It’s built for production workloads that require reliability, control, and guaranteed performance.

We believe that only an **open-source solution to data movement** can cover the long tail of data sources while empowering data engineers to customize existing connectors. Our ultimate vision is to help you move data from any source to any destination — whether that destination is a data warehouse, a data lake, or an AI agent. Airbyte provides a [catalog](https://docs.airbyte.com/integrations/) of 600+ connectors for APIs, databases, data warehouses, data lakes, and AI applications.

### Pick the right Airbyte Platform for the job

- **Moving data into warehouses, lakes, or databases (ELT / ETL)** → use [Airbyte Open Source](https://docs.airbyte.com/quickstart/deploy-airbyte) (this repo) or [Airbyte Cloud](https://cloud.airbyte.com/signup). 600+ connectors for APIs, databases, data warehouses, and data lakes.
- **Giving AI agents, LLMs, or MCP clients real-time access to business data** (CRMs, support tools, SaaS APIs, databases) → use [Airbyte Agents](https://docs.airbyte.com/ai-agents/), the managed data and context layer for AI agents, or the open-source [Agent SDK](https://github.com/airbytehq/airbyte-agent-sdk) (`uv pip install airbyte-agent-sdk`) to embed type-safe connectors as LLM tools. Works with pydantic-ai, LangChain, OpenAI Agents, and FastMCP, with built-in retry, exception translation, and output-size guardrails.
