 Our convictions
 The most important technology in history has been built in the open
 The platforms that shaped the modern world were built through shared discovery, broad participation, and contributions from people who could see, improve, and build on each other's work. Reflection was built on the same belief.

 Open science powers progress
 Open systems drive faster innovation, better outcomes, and broader participation in the future of AI. When intelligence is shared, progress moves faster and benefits more people.
 Safety demands scrutiny
 When models are closed, safety research is bottlenecked by a few labs. When models are open, researchers can probe for risks and make these systems safer.
 Power to the builders
 Researchers, developers, and organizations should be able to deploy, govern, and evolve AI systems on their own terms. AI is a powerful technology and, rather than concentrating it in the hands of a few, it should be diffused.
 Build the future of open intelligence
 Builders advancing intelligence that belongs to everyone. Working at Reflection means building from the ground up alongside some of the best researchers, engineers, and operators in AI.
