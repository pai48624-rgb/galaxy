 Introduction
 Superwhisper turns your voice into clean, formatted text in any app. It runs on macOS, Windows, iOS, and Android.
 Superwhisper turns your voice into clean, formatted text in any app: messages, emails, notes, and code. It records and summarizes meetings too, and with local models nothing you say leaves your device. 
 Superwhisper works offline. Local voice models transcribe without an internet connection. AI formatting offline needs the local S1-mini model in the Message or Mail mode. Other AI processing needs the internet.

 How it works 
 1
 Dictation
 Speak normally (or whisper ). 
 2
 Transcription
 State-of-the-art AI models convert your voice into text, often within seconds. 
 3
 Smart processing
 Optionally, AI enhances your dictation to match your preferred tone, format, and communication style. 
 ​ 
 Key features 
 Intelligent modes
 Use built-in modes for plain transcription or formatted text, or create custom modes with your own AI instructions. Each mode processes your dictation differently, from plain voice-to-text to a full rewrite in your format. 
 Meeting recorder
 Record meetings, including system audio from calls, and get a structured summary with key decisions and action items. Optional speaker separation labels who said what. 
 Write for me
 Describe what you need and the Write for me mode drafts it: dictate a rough outline and get finished text. 
 Context-aware AI
 When enabled, context awareness uses what you’re working on (selected text and clipboard content) to sharpen results. 
 File transcription
 Pro feature. Superwhisper transcribes existing audio and video files: lectures, meetings, interviews. 
 Language support
 Superwhisper supports 100+ languages. Dictate in yours, translate to English on the fly, or use any mode, built-in or custom, in your language.
