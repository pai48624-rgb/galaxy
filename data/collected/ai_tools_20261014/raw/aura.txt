 General
 How do message limits work now?
 All AI models (Grok, GPT, Claude, Gemini) count toward the same usage limit. Pro, Max, and Ultra trials include 20 trial prompts over 3 days. After trial, Pro users get 120 monthly messages (including 20 bonus), Max users get 240 monthly, Ultra users get 560 monthly, and Elite users get 1,080 monthly.
 General
 How does the trial work?
 Pro, Max, and Ultra start with a 3-day trial and 20 trial prompts. A payment method is required to start. You will not be charged if you cancel before the trial ends.
