 About Synthesis
 The math tutor born at SpaceX.
 Synthesis is an AI-powered math tutor that teaches kids ages 5-11 to truly understand math, not just memorize it, through one-on-one lessons designed by expert educators. 
 Over 100,000 students have used Synthesis. It started as a class at the experimental school on the SpaceX campus, and today it is available to families and schools around the world.

 Products
 What Synthesis does
 Synthesis builds learning experiences that help kids become confident, independent problem solvers. We offer three things.
 Tutor 
 Synthesis Tutor
 An AI-powered, adaptive math tutor for kids ages 5-11. It teaches K-5 math topics such as counting, addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, fractions, and algebraic thinking, and goes further for kids who are ready.
 Every lesson gives immediate, patient guidance on each problem and adjusts to the child in real time, so kids feel like they are learning with a real teacher.
 Learn about Synthesis Tutor › 
 Teams 
 Synthesis Teams
 A live, online program for kids ages 8-14. Students from around the world team up to play complex strategy games and hold student-led discussions, guided by live coaches.
 Each week includes a 60-minute game session and a 45-minute discussion session, with times for families in every major time zone. Memberships are $95 per month with no contract.
 Learn about Synthesis Teams › 
 Schools 
 Synthesis for Schools
 Synthesis Tutor is available for classrooms, schools, homeschool pods, and school districts. Teachers get the same adaptive, one-on-one instruction for every student, with progress reporting built in.
 Because the Tutor handles individual pacing, teachers can spend more of their time with the students who need them most.

 Why Synthesis
 What makes Synthesis Tutor different
 Most math tools for kids fall into four groups: worksheet and drill apps, video-lesson platforms, in-person tutoring centers, and general-purpose AI chatbots. Here is how Synthesis compares.
 01 
 A tutor, not a worksheet
 Worksheet and drill apps are digital versions of textbooks. Synthesis Tutor was built from the ground up for the screen, so it gives immediate, tailored guidance on every problem in every lesson instead of simply marking answers right or wrong.
 02 
 Mastery before moving on
 On video-lesson platforms, kids mostly watch. Synthesis checks understanding with micro-assessments throughout every lesson, and a child only moves on once they have shown they have mastered the material. It is continuous assessment, not a one-time test.
 03 
 Designed by educators, not handed to a chatbot
 General-purpose AI chatbots can produce answers, but they are not built to teach young children. Synthesis uses AI where it helps, and a team of expert educators and neuroscientists designs the lessons and reviews every interaction for quality.
 04 
 One-on-one attention for less than $1 a day
 In-person tutoring centers offer personal attention but are expensive and limited to set hours. Synthesis Tutor costs $119 per year for up to 7 kids, is available any time, and never runs out of patience.
