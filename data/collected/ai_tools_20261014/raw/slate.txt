 What Slate does
 Runs longer tasks
 Slate keeps a task alive across exploration, implementation, builds, test failures, and follow-up edits instead of treating each prompt like a fresh start.
 Coordinates parallel work
 It can split independent research, coding, and verification work into focused threads, then bring the useful results back into the main session.
 Manages context deliberately
 Slate tracks what matters, summarizes when needed, and avoids losing the original goal while the agent gathers more codebase state.
 FAQ
 What makes Slate different from other coding agents?
 Slate engineers its own context so you don't have to. It has a built-in compaction algorithm that surfaces the most important information at any time. This allows Slate to stay focused on the task at hand and avoid the dumb zone .
 How do I plan without plan mode?
 Slate plans implicitly. When given a task, Slate will start by researching it, and then present its plan to you. Slate doesn't have plan mode because a good plan is not made by asking 3 questions. A good plan is made by having an agent that has a discussion with you about how to solve a task.
 Is it good?
