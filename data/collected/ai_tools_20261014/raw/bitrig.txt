 Frequently Asked Questions
 What is Bitrig?
 Bitrig is a Mac app for building and shipping native Swift apps with AI. It works on real Xcode projects, compiles them with the full Xcode toolchain and the latest SDKs, and supports every Apple platform: iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, Apple TV, and Vision Pro.
 What do I need to run Bitrig?
 An Apple Silicon Mac running macOS Sequoia or later.
 Can I bring my own agent?
 Yes, and most users do. For Claude and ChatGPT, Bitrig uses the account you’re already signed into on your Mac. For other Agent Client Protocol agents, you connect them in Settings. For either, Bitrig routes requests through the plan you already pay for. Free plans get 10 messages a day. Pro plans get as much as your AI subscription allows.
 What if I don’t have an AI subscription?
 Instead of bringing your subscription you can use credits . Credits work with the latest models from Anthropic, or OpenAI.
 How do I cancel my subscription?
 Cancel any time in Settings or through the Stripe customer portal . You'll keep access to Pro features until the end of your current billing period.
 Can't find your account? Email us at support@bitrig.com and we'll help.
 Which models does Bitrig use?
 Bitrig runs Claude Code and Codex out of the box, using the latest models from Anthropic and OpenAI. Bitrig runs these fully loaded, with existing MCP servers, skills, and conversations. You can also add any agent that speaks Agent Client Protocol, including OpenCode, Cursor, and Grok Build.
 Do I need a paid Apple Developer account?
 A paid Apple Developer Account ($99/year) is required to distribute your app to TestFlight or the App Store. You can build and test apps on your device without one.
 Can I use Bitrig with an existing Xcode project?
 Yes. Open any local Xcode project in Bitrig, or connect your GitHub account and import an existing repository. Either way, Bitrig builds, tests, and runs your existing schemes and targets.
 With GitHub connected, Bitrig can commit and push changes back up, matching your team’s existing workflow.

 About Bitrig
 Bitrig is built by co-creators and longtime members of Apple’s SwiftUI team.
 SwiftUI helped developers build better apps with less code. It lowered the barrier to entry without lowering the standard for quality. For people who prize craft and attention to detail but find traditional native development too difficult, it opened a new door.
 Bitrig is the continuation of that story. AI makes it easier than ever to create software. Bitrig helps you make apps that feel native, polished, and worthy of your taste.
