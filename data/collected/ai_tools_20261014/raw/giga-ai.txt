 FAQ 
 Frequently Asked Questions
 How does Giga AI work?
 Giga reads your entire codebase and explains it to your AI assistant so it stops making mistakes.
 How it works:
 Install the extension and click "Analyze" - Giga scans all your code.
 Giga creates summary files about your project: What components you have
 Your API endpoints
 Your data structure
 Your main functions
 How everything connects
 These summaries get fed to Cursor/Copilot so they understand your code.
 When you change code, Giga updates the summaries automatically.
 Result: Your AI stops hallucinating and gives you code that actually works with your project. Saves builders 20+ hours per month.
 How does Giga AI handle security and privacy?
 Your code stays private. We don't store it.
 We read your code, analyze it, then delete it immediately.
 Everything is encrypted and secure.
 You can exclude sensitive files using .gitignore.
 We don't need your API keys or passwords.
 Your code is safe and never leaves your control.
