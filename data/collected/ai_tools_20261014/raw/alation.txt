 I 
 THE PROBLEM
 AI can quietly (and confidently) break. The problem is figuring out where and why.
 An agent gives you the wrong answer. Is it the data, the context, the agent, two of the three, or all of it at the same time?
 01 _ Bad data reaches the agent Stale, wrong, or drifting data... and the agent acts on it confidently. Detect and remediate before it ships. 
 02 _ The agent misreads context Conflicting definitions, missing semantics. Context is built and tested against real data and real agents, not guessed. 
 03 _ The agent itself is wrong Your prompt is old or wrong. You have too few tools or too many, or they're old. 
 II 
 THE ANSWER IS AIOS
 The Alation Intelligence Operating System ensures your data apps and agents are accurate.
 Because there are so many ways to get the wrong answer, you see whether it was the data , the context , or the agent — and fix the right thing, with the right feedback, to constantly improve.
