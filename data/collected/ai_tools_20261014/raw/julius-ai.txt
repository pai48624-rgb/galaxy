 Frequently asked questions
 How does Julius handle security and compliance? Julius ensures our customer data remains safe and secure. We’re SOC 2 Type II compliant. For more information on data privacy and security, see our privacy policy here .
 Which data sources and warehouses does Julius integrate with? We natively support integrations with Snowflake, BigQuery, MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQL Server, along with other integrations as well. We also support connecting into Google Drive, OneDrive, and Sharepoint for teams.
 Can I build presentations, websites, and videos on any plan? Yes. Every Julius plan, including Free, lets you create presentations, websites, and videos. The Free plan comes with a daily credit allowance. If you want to make more, try more versions, or keep iterating without waiting for your credits to refresh, you’ll need to upgrade to a paid plan.
 How can we manage our Julius subscription? Once subscribed you can make adjustments to your subscription, updating your billing information, and more from your Subscription page . To learn more, check out our docs on Managing your Subscription.
 How can my organization select between Business and Enterprise? The Julius Business tier is meant for younger companies, smaller teams, or larger teams that are looking to self-serve test whether the features of Julius are a proper fit for their organization. Julius Enterprise is built for organizations that are looking to bring on multiple internal teams to Julius, or take advantage of additional security layers such as SSO, Audit logging, fine-grained additional RBAC, or other Enterprise features.
 How does Julius query across my database if I have thousands of tables? As you query your data warehouse, Julius builds a semantic layer of your schema to avoid having to tackle thousands of tables at a time, and only selecting for relevant information to your query. Additionally, users can set connections with manually filtered schemas, include their data dictionary, or create Custom Agents to refine their analytical process.
