 FAQ
 Questions?   We've got answers. 
 What is Make?
 Make is an AI automation platform. You build automated workflows and AI agents visually, connect them to the apps and data you already use, and see everything running in one place. It's built for teams and organizations deploying AI and automation across the business, from a single department to company-wide, with full visibility and control.
 Is there a free plan?
 Yes. The free plan includes enough credits and active scenarios to build and run real automations, with no card required.
 Does Make integrate with the tools we already use?
 Almost certainly. Make connects to 3,500+ apps out of the box, and anything with an API can be added as a custom integration.
 Can I build without a developer?
 Yes. Anyone comfortable thinking through a process — mapping steps, conditions, and outcomes — can build by prompting, dragging and dropping, or working from an AI chat. No coding required; developers can add custom code where it helps.
 How does Make handle security and compliance for larger organizations?
 Make is GDPR compliant and SOC 2 Type II and SOC 3 certified, with encryption, SSO, role-based access and audit logs. Enterprise plans add further controls.
