 Multi Agents for Better Problem-Solving
 TRAE brings every project and task into one workspace. It coordinates Agents in parallel and keeps progress, status, and results clearly in view.
 TRAE understands your vision, plans the development workflow, utilizes the right tools, executes with precision and deploys production-ready code, turning your ideas into software.
 SOLO is our most advanced coding agent. It is truly responsive to your ideas, your context, and your workflow. Delegate tasks and watch it ship complete solutions with the right context and tools. You stay visually in control while AI handles the complexity.
 Built-in agents come with their own expertise right out of the box. Customize your own agents that can handle specific tasks. Your agents can plan, execute, and act as sub-agents to tackle complex challenges together. TRAE also supports an open agent ecosystem where you can share and discover community-built agents.

 Run Projects in Parallel
 TRAE lets you manage projects in one workspace while multiple Agents work in parallel. You can see progress and results at a glance, with less switching and less overhead.
 Build your own agent team
 TRAE gives you a powerful, open agent framework. Start with built-in agents for common tasks, or create custom agents tailored to your specific workflow—define their tools, skills, and logic to make them true specialists. Deploy them as standalone agents or as sub-agents that other agents can call upon. Build an AI team that truly works for you.
 Smart tool integration
 TRAE agents adapt by pulling in exactly what they need, when they need it. Through Model Context Protocol (MCP), agents can access external resources on demand. The built-in preview tab turns browser into a development tool—agents can interact with elements, read console logs, and debug in real-time. Everything responds intelligently to your workflow's changing context.
 More context, more accuracy
 TRAE can deeply understand your development context through your code repositories on the IDE, as well as external information from online searches and documents that you share.You can customize rules to precisely tailor AI behavior to your workflow, enabling it to execute tasks exactly as you envision.
 Predicts your next edit with a single Tab
 CUE understands your intent deeper and anticipates your next move with just one keystroke. Hit Tab to jump to the next change, or apply smart suggestions across multiple lines at once. We've optimized our model to think ahead with you. Just Tab.
