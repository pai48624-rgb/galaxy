 About Vector Institute
 Vector Institute transforms cutting-edge artificial intelligence research into practical solutions that drive Canada’s economic growth and improve lives. As a globally-renowned AI research institute, we bridge the gap between academic discovery and industry adoption through partnerships with leading researchers, businesses, and government organizations.
 Where AI breakthroughs turn into real-world impact
 We will drive excellence and leadership in Canada’s knowledge, creation, and use of artificial intelligence to foster economic growth and improve the lives of Canadians.
 Read more 
 What we do
 Research and Talent
 Vector advances the frontiers of artificial intelligence through world-class research and develops the next generation of AI leaders. Our faculty and researchers tackle pressing challenges in machine learning, deep learning, and AI safety while training exceptional talent through scholarships, university partnerships, and mentorship programs. We ensure Canada’s AI ecosystem has both breakthrough discoveries and skilled professionals to implement them.
