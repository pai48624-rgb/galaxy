 Frequently asked questions 
 What is Ideogram? Ideogram is a generative media platform. You can use purpose-built creative apps, work with leading image and video models, or build your own workflow through one API.
 What can I create with Ideogram's apps? Ideogram apps handle focused production tasks such as removing backgrounds and objects and resizing creative for different formats. Explore all available apps .
 Which image and video models are available? The catalog brings Ideogram models together with models from leading research labs. Availability grows as new models are added to the platform.
 How does the unified API work? One endpoint handles authentication, usage, and billing across supported image and video models. You can switch models by changing the model string in your request.
 Can businesses and creative teams use Ideogram? Yes. Business and Enterprise plans support collaborative workflows, private generation, security requirements, custom models, and production support. Learn about Ideogram Enterprise .
 Is Ideogram 4.0 an open model? Yes. Ideogram 4.0 weights are available for local use and customization under the applicable license. You can also access the same model through Ideogram's platform.
