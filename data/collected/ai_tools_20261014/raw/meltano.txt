 Answers to all your questions
 What is a compute hour, and how do I know how many I need? A compute hour is the time Meltano's managed infrastructure spends actively running your pipelines, extracting data from a source, loading it to a destination, or transforming it. A connector that takes 30 minutes to sync and runs twice a day uses about 30 hours a month. Most small teams with 5–10 connectors syncing daily fall comfortably within the Starter tier (200 hrs/mo). If you're unsure, book a demo and our team will estimate your footprint before you commit.
 What's the difference between Meltano Cloud and Open? Meltano Open is the self-hosted, open-source version, you get 600+ connectors and Git-native pipelines, but you manage your own infrastructure, monitoring, and uptime. Meltano Cloud (Starter, Growth, Scale and Enterprise) is fully managed offering where Meltano runs the infrastructure, handles scaling, monitors pipeline health, and provides engineering support depending on your plan. Open is great for experimentation, but Cloud is built for production teams.
 Can I switch plans as my data needs grow? Yes. Plans are designed to scale with you. From Starter through Enterprise, each tier unlocks more compute hours, more workspaces, and higher levels of engineering support. You can upgrade at any point and your existing pipelines, connectors, and configurations carry over without migration.
 What does "engineering on demand" actually mean? Growth includes 8 hours/month and Scale includes 15 hours/month of direct access to Meltano's engineering team. You can use these hours to get help building custom connectors, debugging pipeline failures, optimising sync performance, or reviewing your architecture. It's embedded engineering support, not a support ticket queue.

## Integrations

[Meltano Hub](https://hub.meltano.com/) is the single source of truth to find any Meltano plugins as well as [Singer](https://singer.io/) taps and targets. Users are also able to add more plugins to the Hub and have them immediately discoverable and usable within Meltano. The Hub is lovingly curated by Meltano and the wider Meltano community.
