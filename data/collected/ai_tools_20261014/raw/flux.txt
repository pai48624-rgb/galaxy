 Video Audio Image Action-Prediction 
 Stylistically diverse beyond just cinematic, with native audio and up to 20 second clips in a single generation. Start from text, an image, or keyframes, and get multiple shots in one take.
 Optional audio. Multilingual speech, effects and ambience generated with the frames.
 Images grounded in the real world. A wide variety of styles, highly-accurate text rendering, and the ability to handle complex prompts.
