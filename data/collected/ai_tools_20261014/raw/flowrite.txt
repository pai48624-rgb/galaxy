 Flowrite x MailMaestro
 Maestro Labs, the company behind MailMaestro , has acquired Flowrite, the AI email writing division of Flow AI.
 The team behind Flowrite will continue their work on Flow AI , while Maestro Labs integrates Flowrite's business into MailMaestro . Flowrite users are welcome to explore MailMaestro's enhanced AI email capabilities.
 The team behind Flowrite continues their focus on Flow AI, an advanced platform for AI teams to evaluate and improve LLM systems effortlessly.

 What's included in the Free plan?
 The free plan allows you to use key features of MailMaestro's AI email assistant - including composing, replying and improving emails with a weekly limit of 3 requests. 
 You also get to enjoy our full functionality for the first 14 days of using our tool, thanks to the free trial.
 What happens when the free trial ends?
 After the free trial ends, you'll get reverted to a "Free" plan, with a limit of 3 AI email assistant requests a week, that you can spend to craft, reply to and improve emails.
 How to upgrade my plan?
 To upgrade your plan, head to your inbox, open MailMaestro and click "Upgrade your plan". Then, follow the instructions on the screen. For more details, head to our support article .
 Do you offer any discounted plans?
 By choosing a yearly plan, you can enjoy 20% discount.
 How do you ensure my data is safe?
 We anonymyse data before sharing it with LLMs, and use cooperation plans that prevents LLMs from training on your private data.
 What is your cancellation policy?
 Users have a two-week free trial to test the tool.
 Yearly subscriptions are non-refundable after purchase.
 Monthly plans are available for those who prefer flexibility.
 Monthly and yearly subscriptions are automatically renewed.
 To cancel a subscription, users can manage their plan directly in their account settings before the next billing cycle.
 For any questions, users can contact us at feedback@maestrolabs.com .
