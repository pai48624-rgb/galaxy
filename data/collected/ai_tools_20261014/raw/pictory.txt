 What is doc to video AI?
 Doc to video AI is software that turns a written document, such as a report, manual or guide, into a video. Pictory’s doc to video AI reads the document, pulls out the key points, writes a script and builds scenes with visuals, a voiceover and captions you can edit.
 Your own charts and images come with it. Charts, tables and pictures in the document are reused in the scenes they belong to, so the video shows your data.
 You check the script before anything is built. It takes a PDF up to 50 MB, and a Word or Google Doc works once you save it as one. It runs online in your browser and is free to try.
