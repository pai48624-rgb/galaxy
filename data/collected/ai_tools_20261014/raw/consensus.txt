 Automate Literature Review with Deep Search
 Turn days of literature review into a few minutes. Consensus Deep Search builds a comprehensive search strategy, expanding key terms, identifying conflicting arguments, and exploring the citation graph.
 What are the competing theoretical explanations for the placebo effect in pain management?
 Compare randomized vs observational evidence on intermittent fasting

 Try Medical mode
 Narrow your results to the highest-quality medical sources — about 50,000 clinical guidelines and 8M articles from the top 1,000 medical journals for quick, trusted clinical answers.
 Workup for persistent fever with negative cultures
 Guideline-based management of atrial fibrillation with CKD
 SGLT2 inhibitors vs GLP-1 agonists for renal outcomes in type 2 diabetes
 Use filters with natural language
 Specify timeframes, populations, designs, and more directly in your prompt — and Consensus automatically applies the right filters for your search.
 Controlled studies only evaluating cognitive training programs for memory improvement
 How has the scientific consensus on dietary fat evolved since the 1990s?
 Large human studies (n > 1,000) examining long-term outcomes of intermittent fasting
 See where the research agrees
 Ask a clear yes-or-no research question and the Consensus Meter instantly shows how much the evidence agrees or disagrees — giving you a fast, visual read on the state of the research.

 Better science is how we build a better world
 To create new knowledge, you must first understand what’s already known. But humanity’s hard-won insights are locked inside millions of research papers. Navigating this maze is overwhelming for researchers and purely out of reach for anyone outside academia.
 We’re building the best way to search, synthesize, and understand scientific knowledge—so researchers can get back to science , not searches, and everyone can access evidence, not opinion. 
 We want to see a world with <u> superhuman researchers, and a generation of citizen scientists </u> . This is the world we’re creating with Consensus.

 Our Story
 Consensus was founded in 2021 by Christian Salem and Eric Olson, two Division 1 athlete teammates from families of researchers and teachers. They bonded over a shared love for science but felt like outsiders looking in.
 Consensus was started to ensure that no one feels like an outsider to science , and to make research easier to find, understand, and trust. What began as a remote-first company, Consensus now calls San Francisco home.
