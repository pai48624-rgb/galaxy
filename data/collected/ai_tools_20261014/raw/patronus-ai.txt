 Patronus AI is a research lab developing training data and simulated environments for frontier AI.
 Research 
 Building Worlds
That Teach AI
 Our research connects a deep investigation of model failures with new methods for developing high-quality tasks and environments for reinforcement learning.
 Through scalable world modeling and synthetic data generation, we aim to extend human expertise across more training experiences while preserving the standards of real-world work.
