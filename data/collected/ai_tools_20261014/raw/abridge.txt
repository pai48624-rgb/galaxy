 Restoring Humanity
 Back to Healthcare
 Abridge gives clinicians a single intelligence layer—so prep is done before they walk in, documentation is completed during the visit, and everything downstream is handled when they leave.
 Customer Stories
 CARE WORKFLOW
 Intelligence for care teams
 From start of visit to follow-up
 Transform clinical conversations into documentation, insights, and actions for clinicians, RCM, nurses, IT, and payers.
 PRE-VISIT
 Prepared before the conversation
 Pulls together patient history, prior visits, and relevant context—so clinicians walk in informed, not catching up.
 ENCOUNTER
 Present during the encounter
 Captures the conversation in real time while surfacing clinical decision support, identifying care gaps, and supporting orders—so clinicians stay with the patient, not the screen.
 POST-VISIT
 Handled after the visit
 Finalized notes, coding specificity, orders, and patient summaries—ready for review, billing, and follow-up. The work is done before the clinician sits down.
