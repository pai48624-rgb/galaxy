 What is Base44? 
 Base44 is a no-code AI platform for building apps, websites and AI agents — no coding required. Describe what you want, and Base44 generates the structure, design and logic for you. It handles the technical side so you can focus on the idea.
Beyond building, Base44 gives you access to Superagents — AI agents that automate workflows, manage data and connect to your tools. They work alongside what you build, keeping things running even when you're not.
 How does Base44 work? 
 Base44 uses vibe coding to build apps, websites, agents and more. Describe your idea in plain language — as a rough thought or a detailed spec. Base44 generates the code, structure, design and logic for your app or website. From there, keep iterating by chatting with the AI until everything works the way you want.
 How much does it cost? 
 Base44 has a free plan — no credit card needed to start. The free plan includes monthly credits to build and test your first apps. Paid plans start at $16/month (billed annually) and unlock more credits, advanced features and team capabilities. See full details on the pricing page .
 Do I need coding experience? 
 No. Describe what you need in plain language and Base44 handles the rest.
 Which AI no-code platform is best to build an app? 
 The best AI no-code platform is the one that can turn your idea into a fully functional app — not just a static design — without requiring any coding experience. As you evaluate your options, here are the key factors to consider:
 Full-stack generation — Does the AI handle the backend (data storage, authentication, logic) automatically, or just the UI?
 Iteration speed — Can you refine the app by chatting with the AI, or do you need to manually configure changes?
 Built-in hosting — Is your app instantly live after building, or do you need to set up separate deployment?
 Integrations — Does it connect to the tools you already use, like Slack, Google Calendar, or HubSpot?
 Scalability — Can it grow from a simple internal tool to an enterprise product?
 Base44 covers all of these out of the box. You describe your idea in plain language, and the AI generates a working app — complete with design, logic, and a backend — in minutes. From there, you can iterate, connect integrations, and go live instantly with built-in hosting.
Check out our step-by-step guide on how to build an app with AI to see exactly how it works.
 Can I build a complete website with Base44? 
 Yes. Base44's AI website builder lets you generate a complete, fully functional website in minutes — just describe what you need in plain language. The AI handles layout, design, and content for you, with no coding or setup required.
 You can build a wide range of website types, including:
 Business websites and landing pages — designed to convert visitors into paying customers, with clear messaging and calls to action
 Portfolios and personal sites — showcase your work, skills, or brand with a professional look that makes a lasting impression
 Online stores and e-commerce sites — sell products or services with a fully functional storefront, from product pages to checkout
 Internal tools and client portals — give your team or clients a dedicated space to access data, track progress, and get things done
 Once your site is ready, Base44 provides built-in hosting and custom domain support — so publishing takes one click. You can keep refining your site by chatting with the AI until it looks and works exactly the way you want.
 What kinds of apps can I build? 
 Builders use Base44 for internal tools and dashboards, developer productivity apps, customer portals and CRMs, education and training platforms, health and wellness trackers, and a lot more. Browse the Use Cases library for ideas and ready-to-build examples.
 What integrations does Base44 support? 
 Base44 connects to the tools you already use — Google Calendar, Gmail, Slack, Notion, HubSpot, Salesforce and more. You can also connect to any external service via API. Common capabilities like sending emails, managing data and AI-generated content are built in and ready to use without extra setup.

 Frequently

 What is Base44 and how does it work? 
 Base44 is a no-code AI platform that uses vibe coding to build fully-functional apps and websites. You describe what you need in plain language, and Base44 builds the full thing: frontend, backend, database, user auth, payments, all of it. The apps run on real production infrastructure, not a sandbox. Integrate it easily to all your daily tools, such as Gmail, Slack, Notion, Google Drive, Hubspot, Salesforce, etc. Keep your apps and websites private, share with your team, or publish to the web.
 Do I own my app, and can I export the code? 
 Yes. You own everything you build on Base44: your code, your data, your users. Two-way GitHub sync exports the full source code to your own repo at any time. Nothing is locked to the platform.
 How much does it cost to use Base44? 
 You can start building with Base44 for free, with every core feature included. Paid plans scale by credits and add tools, integrations, and support: Starter for your own projects, Builder for teams stepping up, Pro for complex apps, and Elite for builders going all in. Choose monthly or yearly billing, with a 20% discount on yearly billing. Enterprise pricing is custom — talk to sales for a quote.
 How do I know which plan is right for me? 
 The Free plan is a full free AI app maker with every core feature, and you can ship complete apps on it. When you hit your monthly credit limit or want premium integrations, you can upgrade to a paid plan. Most app creators land on Starter. Teams shipping multiple apps tend to land on Builder or Pro. Elite is for people running their main product or business on Base44.
 Can I cancel or change my plan at any time? 
 Yes. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel any time from your account settings. Changes take effect at the next billing cycle. Your apps stay live, your data stays in your workspace.
 Does Base44 offer discounts on annual plans? 
 Yes. Annual billing saves 20% on every paid plan (Starter, Builder, Pro, and Elite). The discount applies to the monthly price multiplied by twelve, charged once a year.
 What forms of payment do you accept? 
 All major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover). Enterprise customers can pay by invoice or ACH transfer.
 Are there any additional fees beyond the plan price? 
 No hidden fees. Your plan covers monthly credits, included integrations, and the support level for your tier. Third-party services you connect to your apps (Stripe, SendGrid, OpenAI, etc.) bill you directly on their own pricing.
 Do you offer discounts for students, non-profits, or special organizations? 
 Yes. We offer education and non-profit pricing on a case-by-case basis. Sign up with your student email to verify your status or reach out to support with details about your org and what you're building, and we'll see what fits.
 Where can I learn more about building with Base44? 
 The fastest way is to start building — the Free plan gives you full access as an AI app maker and you can ship your first app or website in an afternoon. For deeper AI app development reading, see Base44 docs or browse the community app templates gallery .
